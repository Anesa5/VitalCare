What we treat

Our cardiology team covers the full range of heart conditions, from first-time screening through ongoing management of chronic disease.

  • Chest pain & cardiac events
  • Traumatic injury
  • Severe allergic reactions
  • Stroke symptoms
  • Breathing difficulty
  • Acute abdominal pain

Our approach

Triage begins the moment you arrive, with our team trained to move the most critical cases straight to the resuscitation bay. Once stabilized, you're handed directly to the relevant specialist team — cardiology, neurology or trauma surgery — so there's no second wait for the right care.
