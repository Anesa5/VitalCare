What we treat

Our cardiology team covers the full range of heart conditions, from first-time screening through ongoing management of chronic disease.

  • Coronary artery disease
  • Arrhythmia & pacemaker management
  • Heart failure management
  • Congenital heart conditions
  • Hypertension management
  • Post-cardiac event rehabilitation

Our approach

Every new patient starts with a full diagnostic workup — echocardiogram, stress testing and bloodwork reviewed together, not in isolation. For patients needing intervention, our catheterization lab operates around the clock, and your cardiologist stays involved through recovery and rehab rather than handing you off.
